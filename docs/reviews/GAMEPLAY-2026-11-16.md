# Gameplay review, 2026-11-16: decisions

The user and Claude went through the gameplay together and agreed the changes below. They fall into two groups. "Build now"
has the user's go-ahead. "Decided, build later" and "Noted" are not to be built until the user says so.

The evidence comes from `tools/review/picks.ts`: 264 Hard-vs-Hard matches, every Lord pairing.
- Militia were deployed 11 times out of 5,302 deploys.
- The 150-gold tier made up 1.8% of deploys.
- The Dragon was unlocked 174 times and deployed 5 times.
- Hard took the highest rarity on offer 81% of the time, and the Legendary 80% of the time.

## Build now

### 1. The counter wheel (the single counter rule)

**Classes.** Every unit belongs to exactly one of 8 classes. Lords take the class of their body or mount. Each class
beats the next one around the ring and is beaten by the one before it:

**Swarm → Giant → Line → Cavalry → Ranged → Air → Siege → Magic → Swarm**

| Class | Beats (why) | Beaten by |
|---|---|---|
| Swarm | Giant: many bodies drag it down, and each huge hit kills only one man | Magic (splash) |
| Giant | Line: tramples a packed formation | Swarm |
| Line | Cavalry: braced pikes and shields | Giant |
| Cavalry | Ranged: rides down the archers | Line |
| Ranged | Air: shoots fliers down | Cavalry |
| Air | Siege: dives crews who can't aim upward | Ranged |
| Siege | Magic: outranges and splashes fragile casters | Air |
| Magic | Swarm: splash on packed weak men | Siege |

**Unit assignment (first pass, to be refined in tuning):**
- **Swarm:** Militia, Skeleton Spearmen, Spear Levy.
- **Giant:** Stone Golem, Bone Golem, Sand Golem, War Elephant.
- **Line:** Pikemen, Knights, Berserkers, Grave Knights, Scimitar Guard.
- **Cavalry:** Cavaliers, Mamluk Lancers, Wraith Riders.
- **Ranged:** Archers, Bone Archers, Camel Archers.
- **Air:** Pegasus Knights, Wyvern Riders, Dragon, Bone Drake, Bound Djinn.
- **Siege:** Ballista, Trebuchet, Corpse Catapult.
- **Magic:** Mages, Clerics, Grave Acolytes, Djinn-Binders, Sun Priests. Healers count as Magic for the wheel.
- **Lords:** the class of their body:
  - Marshal: Line;
  - Archmage: Magic;
  - Warden: Line;
  - Skylady: Air;
  - Lich-Priest: Magic;
  - Sultana: Cavalry, if she is mounted (check the model and data);
  - Death Knight: Cavalry, if mounted;
  - Ossuary Warden: Line, or Giant if his body is giant-sized;
  - Drake Rider: Air;
  - Grand Vizier: Magic;
  - Rajah: Giant (the elephant);
  - Roc Rider: Air.

  The implementer checks each Lord's mount or body and records the result in `DESIGN.md`.

**Rules:**
- **One number.** A counter deals **×1.5 damage** to the class it beats (`COUNTER_PCT = 150`, one constant). Every
  other pairing is neutral. If playtests feel flat, the planned fallback is a second, smaller bonus (×1.25) against the
  class two steps ahead. It is not built yet.
- **Removed:** the weapon triangle (sword > axe > lance) and the per-unit tag bonuses that the wheel replaces (Pikemen
  ×2.5 vs mounted, Ballista ×2 vs Large, Archers ×1.25 vs fliers). Legendary and affix bonuses such as "Slaying",
  Kingsbane and Lord passives stay. They are items and choices, not the base rule.
- **Kept:** armour, magic resistance, splash and "melee can't hit fliers" stay as unit stats and traits. They must be
  tuned so they never contradict the wheel; for example, Line armour must not make Magic useless against it.
- **Shown everywhere.** Every unit card, tooltip and the Lord pick show the class icon and "Beats X · Weak to Y". The old
  derived "Strong vs / Countered by" lists are replaced by the wheel. The wheel needs a one-screen explainer in the help.

**Known gap (noted, not built):** the Sun Caliphate has no Siege unit, so it has no unit that beats Magic. It needs a
siege unit later. Until then its tuning has to cover for that.

### 2. Deploy limit by weight (chaff fix 1)

The deploy limit counts squads, whatever they cost, so a 100-gold squad uses up a whole deploy. The new rule:
- Each unit has a **deploy weight** set by its cost: **100 gold = ½**, **150–300 = 1**, **400+ = 1½**.
- Each player gets **2 deploy points a round**. The Marshal's passive and "of Command" still add to it, in the same
  units: +1 deploy = +1 point.
- In code this is integer half-points: weights 1, 2 or 3 against a budget of 4 (the Marshal 6).
- The UI shows "Deploys left: 1½", and the shop shows each card's weight.

### 3. Swarm squads level by adding soldiers (chaff fix 2)

- A Swarm squad's level-up adds soldiers (about +25% of its base squad size per level, e.g. Militia 12 → 15 → 18 …)
  instead of +25% HP and +20% damage per soldier.
- Levelling chaff thickens the wall.

Chaff fixes 3 (a special job for chaff) and 4 (merge or cut Militia) are covered by the wheel: Swarm beats Giants.

### 4. Item rework (item fixes 1 and 2)

- **Equal-power offers.** The three items in one offer share the same rarity and power budget and differ in kind:
  - the weapon is offence (damage, crit, attack speed, burn, slaying);
  - the armour is defence (life, resists, thorns, revive);
  - the relic is utility (speed, range, frost, command, leech).

  Rarity still rises with the round, using the schedule in DESIGN §5 but applied to all three items at once. Every
  3rd round, all three items are Legendary. The choice becomes "what does my army need against this enemy?", not
  "take the biggest number".
- **Narrow, build-defining Legendaries.** Each legendary power only shines in the right army. Kingsbane, for example,
  is huge only against Lords and giants. Tie them to classes where it reads naturally: Hydra Volley for Ranged,
  Gravebloom for a melee or Swarm army, Warhorn for Swarm or Line. A Legendary that doesn't fit your army is a
  genuine pass. Each tooltip shows "Best on: <classes>".
- **Measure it.** With `tools/review/picks.ts`, the share of picks that take a given slot or kind should spread out,
  and no single legendary should be taken more than about 60% of the times it is offered.

Item fixes 3 ("Salvage" for gold, or two Rares instead of one Legendary) and 4 (affixes with drawbacks) are not part of
this pass.

### Balance gates for this pass

Re-run all of these on Hard and Normal before shipping:
- `npm run tournament`, all Lords within 45–55% at Hard;
- faction and Lord tables;
- match length (8–9 rounds on average, rarely past 12);
- the tall-vs-wide gap;
- `tools/review/chaos.ts`;
- `picks.ts`. Every unit should see real use: no unit below about 2% of deploys within its faction, and Militia in
  particular should now be bought.

The bots must understand the wheel: they buy counters to the classes they saw last round, and they place each class
against what it beats.

## Decided, build later (waiting for the user's go-ahead)

### Relocation (Mechabellum-style)

**Built 2026-11-18** (DESIGN §2; `docs/BALANCE.md` "Current report"). As decided below, with three details settled in
the build: turning on the spot is free for every squad; moving a relocated squad home gives the relocation back; and
with nothing left to unlock, Muster is still offered and costs nothing. The optional item is the Rare+ relic suffix
"of the March" (one relocation a round, at most two).

- **Locked:** squads deployed in earlier rounds are locked in place.
- **Free:** the Lord, squads bought this round (until the battle starts), and turning a squad on the spot.
- **0 free moves per round.**
- **Muster** is taken **instead of** that round's unit unlock, and lets you move up to 3 older squads that round.
- **Optional:** an item that grants one move a round.
- **Selling is not a loophole.** An older squad refunds 0 when sold, so selling and rebuying it costs full price, a
  deploy, and its levels and items.
- **Intel:** after each battle, mark which enemy squads moved. The bots must plan for the long term and take Muster
  when their layout is countered.

- A wide/deep formation choice to replace facing, which measures as noise.
- Make the river matter (a slower "hard river" with fixed crossings), or keep it as scenery.
- Teach the speed stagger: a "your squads arrive 4 s apart" hint.
- A ranked ladder or Elo among friends on the server.
