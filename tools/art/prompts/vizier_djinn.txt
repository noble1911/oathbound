A small bound smoke djinn familiar: only the upper body of a little muscular genie with deep blue skin, a bald head with pointed ears, a short black beard and glowing gold eyes, arms crossed over the bare chest, gold bracers and a gold collar, and below the waist no legs: the body tapers into one single thick solid twisting column of blue-teal smoke that narrows to a point at the very bottom. Solid opaque stylised smoke, no thin wisps, no loose puffs, nothing detached. Front view.
