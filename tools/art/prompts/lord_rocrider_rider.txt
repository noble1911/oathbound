The Roc Rider, a veiled archer-warrior woman of a sun caliphate: a slender athletic woman with a turquoise head veil and a thin gold face chain, long dark braids, a fitted cuirass of fine gold lamellar scales over a white silk tunic, gold shoulder guards, a leather quiver strap across the chest, a vivid magenta sash at the waist and a vivid magenta half-cloak falling from the shoulders down the back, gold bracers, loose white riding trousers and soft leather boots, legs set well apart.
