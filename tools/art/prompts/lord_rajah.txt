The Gilded Rajah, a mighty warlord of a sun caliphate: a powerfully built broad man with a thick black braided beard and a fierce face, a gold pointed helmet wrapped in a red and gold striped turban with a turquoise jewel, a long coat of burnished gold scale armour to the knees with round gold shoulder plates, a wide jewelled gold collar with turquoise stones, gold vambraces, a wide vivid magenta waist sash with gold tassels and a vivid magenta cloak hanging from the shoulders down the back, loose white trousers tucked into gold-plated boots, legs clearly separated, proud and commanding.
