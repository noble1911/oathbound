The Grand Vizier, an old sorcerer and lord of a sun caliphate: a tall lean elderly man with a long white beard and a stern hawkish face, a very large white and gold striped turban with a turquoise jewel and a gold sun pin, a long layered court robe of deep turquoise brocade with gold sun embroidery over a white under-robe, a wide gold jewelled collar, a long open vivid magenta outer coat with gold trim and a vivid magenta sash at the waist, wide sleeves ending at the wrists so both hands are clearly visible, gold rings, pointed gold slippers visible below the robe hem.
