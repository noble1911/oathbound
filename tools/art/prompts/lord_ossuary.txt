The Ossuary Colossus, a hulking undead giant built of fused bones and iron plate: a huge broad-shouldered heavy humanoid with very thick arms and legs made of bundled thick bones bound with iron bands and rusted chains, riveted dark iron armour plates on the shoulders, forearms and shins, a horned iron great helm over a large skull face with green glowing eye sockets, an open exposed rib cage wrapped in heavy chains with bright green soul-fire burning inside it, a row of small skulls on the iron belt, a ragged vivid magenta loincloth hanging at the front and a torn vivid magenta half-cape on one shoulder, big bony fists, wide flat feet, legs clearly separated.
