A bound djinn, a towering muscular genie spirit: blue-grey smoke-coloured skin, a bald head with pointed ears and a braided black beard, glowing gold eyes, heavy gold binding bracers on the wrists and a gold collar with a broken chain, a bare broad chest, a vivid magenta cloth sash wound around the waist, and instead of legs the lower body tapers into one single thick swirling column of pale blue-grey smoke that narrows to a point at the very bottom, floating.
