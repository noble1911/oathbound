A campaign map of one battlefield seen straight from above, drawn as a period woodcut map: a river crossing the middle from left to right with one stone bridge and two fords, a square stone keep at the top edge and another at the bottom edge, clumps of trees, low hills drawn with hachure lines, a ruined tower, a small compass rose in a corner. The top keep flies a small madder red pennant; everything else is black line on cream. Even, calm, mostly empty paper. Tall portrait composition, 2:3.
