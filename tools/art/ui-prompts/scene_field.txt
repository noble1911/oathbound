A wide battlefield seen from a low hill at dusk: two hosts drawn up facing each other across a shallow river with a stone bridge in the middle distance. On the left a host under ultramarine blue banners in front of a square stone keep; on the right a host under madder red banners in front of its own keep. Ranks of pikes, a few riders, tall banners, low hills and a dark wood behind. The sky is carved in long horizontal lines and the upper middle third is calm and nearly empty. Wide landscape composition, 3:2.
