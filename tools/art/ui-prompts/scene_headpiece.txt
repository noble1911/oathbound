A printer's headpiece ornament, a long horizontal strip as at the top of a chapter in an early printed book: two lines of tiny soldiers with pikes and banners march towards each other from the left and the right and meet at two crossed swords in the centre; the left host's banners are black, the right host's banners are madder red. The strip sits in the middle of the image with empty cream paper above and below. Wide landscape composition, 3:2.
